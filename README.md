# BeagleSteps.social

BeagleStep.social is a social plateform to share your your trip and journey. It is fully build in the AT ecosystem.
All your data (trip, steps, pictures) are stored in your own PDFS. Your data will always keep yours !
It's inspired by bluesky and OpenStep (https://github.com/TheoLechemia/openstep) an opensource and self-hosted travel book application.

It is for now a _beta_ version with only a frontend and not Appview
